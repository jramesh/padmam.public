Padmam Privacy Policy

Effective 10 November 2026

In short
  • Padmam listens to the microphone only to count your chants, on your phone. Audio is never sent anywhere, and is saved on your phone only if you turn on recording for a chanting step.
  • Your practice history stays on your phone. There are no accounts, no analytics and no ads.
  • We (the developer) receive no data about you.

Who we are

Padmam is a yoga and meditation companion app for Android, made by an independent developer. For any question about this policy or your data, email appdev.ramesh@gmail.com.

Microphone

During a chanting step (for example "AUM Chanting"), Padmam uses the microphone to count your chants. The sound is analysed as it arrives, entirely on your phone, and is then discarded, unless you have turned on recording for that step (below). Padmam never uploads audio. The microphone is on only while a chanting step is running, and you can deny the permission and count chants by tapping instead.

Om recordings (optional, off by default)

To help check a chant count that looks wrong, you can turn on "Record Oms" for a chanting step when customising a routine. Only then, Padmam saves the audio of that step while it runs, including the app's own bell and spoken counts. The practice screen shows "Recording" while it does.

  • Recordings are kept in the app's private storage on your phone, only for the 5 most recent sessions of each recorded step; older ones are deleted automatically.
  • Deleting a session deletes its recordings. Turning recording off stops new ones, and uninstalling the app deletes them all.
  • Recordings are not included in history exports or in Android's device backup.
  • A recording leaves your phone only if you export it yourself, to a place you choose, from that session's Om report. Padmam never sends it anywhere.

What is stored on your phone

To show your history and streaks, Padmam keeps these on your phone, in the app's private storage:

  • Practice sessions: which routine, when it started and ended, and whether it was completed.
  • For each step: whether it was completed or skipped, and for chanting steps the chant count, any manual corrections, and for each chant its time, length, pitch and how steady it was. These are numbers measured on your phone, never audio.
  • Sessions you add by hand, your choices for customising routines, routines you create, and your reminder setting.
  • Om recordings, only for chanting steps you chose to record (see above).

This data never leaves your phone through Padmam, except files you export yourself. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

Backups you control

  • Export and import: you can save your history to a file you choose (for example in Google Drive or Downloads) and import it later. Padmam writes only to the place you pick.
  • Android backup: if Android's backup is turned on in your phone's settings, Android may include Padmam's history (never audio recordings) in your Google device backup, so it can be restored on a new phone. This is managed by Android and Google under your settings and Google's privacy policy; Padmam's developer has no access to it.

Spoken step names

Padmam speaks the name of each step using your phone's text-to-speech service. Only the step name (for example "Patangasana") is passed to it. That service is provided by your phone's maker or by Google and works under their own privacy terms.

Reminders

If you turn on the daily reminder, Padmam schedules it on your phone with Android's notification system. It is created and shown entirely on your phone; nothing is sent to a server.

What we do not do

  • No account or sign-in.
  • No analytics, tracking, advertising or advertising IDs.
  • No selling or sharing of data with anyone.
  • No location, contacts, photos or other personal data.

Children

Padmam is intended for adults and is not directed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
